Daniel Hagenimana | WDD 130

Daniel Hagenimana is a dynamic Rwandan entrepreneur, author, and business advisor recognized for his contributions to corporate leadership and youth empowerment in East Africa. Born on October 23, 1998, in Nyanza, Rwanda, he has rapidly established himself as a versatile business leader based in Kigali. He is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of UHDSS Group, a prominent umbrella organization that manages five distinct business ventures: UHDSS Education Agency, Top Skills, Universal Brokers, Universal Certification, and Invest in Rwanda. Through these entities, he drives innovation across educational consultancy, professional training, and investment promotion. Beyond corporate management, Hagenimana is a dedicated author and mentor. He wrote the acclaimed motivational book “The Journey to Success”, which focuses on self-discovery and strategic personal growth. An MBA candidate at Plymouth Marjon University, he frequently utilizes his academic and practical expertise to speak at business forums, providing strategic advisory services in change management and market analysis. Hagenimana remains committed to fostering the next generation of African entrepreneurs by bridging the gap between education and sustainable business development.
