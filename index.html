Professional Biography Daniel Hagenimana is a Rwandan entrepreneur, business executive, and technology-driven innovator with experience in international education, business management, institutional partnerships, and digital transformation. He is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Universal HD Solution Service (UHDSS), established in 2020, and the driving force behind ScholarVoya, an education technology platform designed to transform how students discover their talents, select careers, access education, and prepare for opportunities in the global workforce. His entrepreneurial journey is rooted in a personal understanding of the challenges young people face when choosing educational pathways without adequate career guidance or reliable information about future employment opportunities. At the age of 17, Daniel experienced the challenge of reconsidering his own academic direction, moving away from an initial interest in chemistry toward business and technology. This experience contributed to his determination to build solutions that help students make better-informed educational and career decisions. Through UHDSS and ScholarVoya, he seeks to connect education, artificial intelligence, career development, and international opportunities in a single accessible ecosystem. 2. Founder and Visionary Behind ScholarVoya One of Daniel's major entrepreneurial initiatives is the development of ScholarVoya, an education technology platform operated under UHDSS. ScholarVoya represents the evolution of UHDSS from a conventional international education services business into an integrated technology-enabled education ecosystem. The platform is designed to help students make educational choices based on their interests, abilities, financial circumstances, career objectives, and long-term aspirations. Core Innovations AI-Powered Career Guidance ScholarVoya uses personalized assessments to help students identify suitable educational pathways and professional opportunities. The system considers individual interests, talents, academic background, aspirations, and other relevant circumstances. International Education Access The platform provides access to educational opportunities internationally, with a reported catalog exceeding 200,000 academic programs across more than 2,000 institutions. Language Learning and Test Preparation Students can access language learning support and preparation services for internationally recognized English proficiency assessments, including IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, and Duolingo English Test. Personalized Academic Support ScholarVoya brings together academic planning, admission guidance, scholarship discovery, application support, and AI-assisted services. Institutional and Agency Collaboration The platform supports collaboration among students, parents, secondary schools, recruitment agencies, and higher education institutions. Long-Term Career Development Its broader vision extends beyond securing university admission to supporting better educational decisions and improved alignment between academic training and labor market demands. Through ScholarVoya, Daniel aims to make high-quality career guidance and international educational information more accessible, particularly to students in emerging markets. 5.
